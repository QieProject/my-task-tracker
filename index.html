Calendar Settings

This is optional. If you leave it empty, events go to your default Google Calendar.

To get a Calendar ID:

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer (or use Desktop site on your phone).
  2. Create a new calendar for each category (optional, but this gives each category its own colour).
  3. Go to Settings, choose the calendar, and scroll to Integrate calendar.
  4. Copy the Calendar ID and paste it below.

How to use

  • Tap Add New Task to add a task.
  • Use Finish, Edit and Delete to manage your tasks.
  • Tap Add to Calendar to send a task to Google Calendar.
  • The red, yellow and blue boxes at the top show what is overdue, due today, or due soon.
  • Your tasks are saved in this browser only. If you clear your browser data, they will be lost.
